Warning Signs for HVAC Maintenance

The sooner you notice these red flags, the easier and cheaper the problem is to resolve. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • You simply have no idea what condition your equipment is actually in
  • Cooling or heating that takes noticeably longer to reach the setpoint
  • A newer unit with warranty terms that call for regular professional service
  • A breakdown last season that arrived with zero warning
  • Musty air when the fan starts, or a filter that has been in far too long
  • Energy bills creeping up without any change in your habits
  • A startup sound that has changed since last year
  • Nobody has opened up the system in over a year

How to Prevent HVAC Maintenance Problems

A few simple habits now, like regular filter changes and seasonal tune-ups, can add years to your system's life. Start with these practical measures our team relies on:

  • Trim plants back from the outdoor unit and give the coil a gentle rinse every spring
  • Book spring and fall visits in the shoulder seasons, before the rush and before the extremes
  • If you have gas or other fuel-burning equipment, test your CO detectors every month
  • Clear the condensate drain early in cooling season, before algae has a chance to grow
  • Keep supply and return registers open in every room, even the ones you rarely use
  • Swap the filter every one to three months, the most valuable habit a homeowner can build
  • Jot down any unusual sounds or run times, since they help your technician diagnose early
